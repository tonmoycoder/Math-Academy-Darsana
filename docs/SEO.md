## 1 · SEO goal and reader

Help a student or parent arrive from a Bengali math-learning query, get an accurate and useful answer, and—only if relevant—find the academy's contact route. The site must be readable with assistive technology, crawlable without client-side rendering, and accurate on a phone. Do not manufacture keyword volume, student outcomes, reviews, or search rankings.

## 2 · Current route structure

| Route | Purpose | Indexing intent |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Local academy landing page with four blog cards | Indexable canonical homepage |
| `/blog/` | Hub for all ten complete articles; featured row mirrors the four homepage cards | Indexable collection page |

## 4 · On-page article requirements

1. One main intent per URL; write the direct answer in the opening paragraph.
2. Use a useful Bengali `title`, one visible `h1`, accurate `desc`, a primary `keyword`, and a unique slug. Keep titles concise (target ≤60 characters) and descriptions around ≤150 characters; these are editing targets, not guarantees of how a search engine will display a snippet.
3. Use numbered, descriptive headings and examples. Use tables/lists only when they clarify the material.
4. Include exactly three concise, useful FAQ pairs; the build emits FAQ structured data from the same draft answers.
5. Link to two or three relevant articles with descriptive Bengali anchors. Check the destination against the ten current slugs.
6. Cite primary NCTB/board documents near year-specific exam claims; identify the year and add an update reminder.
7. Avoid keyword stuffing, false certainty, promised marks, guessed exam questions, fabricated reviews, or unsupported school endorsement.
8. Keep changing batch capacity, filled seats, schedule and fees out of static article prose; when relevant use approved data tokens and the dynamic source documented in `docs/CONTENT.md`.

## 5 · Technical SEO generated by the build

`python3 _src/build.py --publish` creates the deployable output. `_src/build.py` owns and generates:

- canonical URLs and `bn-BD` document language;
- unique page title/description and social metadata for the hub and each article;
- Organization/Person information plus CollectionPage/ItemList, Article, FAQPage and Breadcrumb JSON-LD where appropriate;
- `/sitemap.xml` from the homepage, hub, and the ten current article slugs;
- `/llms.txt` page inventory and `/llms-full.txt` readable rendered content;
- `robots.txt` host references and themed `404.html`.

The homepage blog teaser uses four full article summaries and the exact CTA **«আরও ব্লগ দেখুন»** to `/blog/`. Confirm that link works in the hosted build. The offline single-file output cannot provide the separate blog routes; it must not claim that those routes are local files.
