# STORYLINES — Math Academy Darsana launch film

## Assumptions (direct-file request; no approval pause)

- 25 seconds, 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps.
- Bengali on-screen copy; no voiceover.
- No user-supplied/licensed music or authenticated media provider is available in this environment, so the MP4 will be silent. No music has been fabricated or sourced without permission.
- Use only the real supplied teacher portrait/logo and claims already present in the repository. Do not show student faces, fake testimonials, invented marks, fake scarcity, or a mock website interface.

## 1. Recommended — An equation becomes an idea

**Device/form:** one SSC-style sum transforms into the pairing proof, then resolves to the academy's teaching approach. A warm paper/ink/red visual world uses one moving number pair as the thread.

**First two seconds:** “১ থেকে ১০০—যোগ করবেন কীভাবে?” appears beside the actual expression `1 + 2 + … + 100`.

**Beats:** question → pair the first and last numbers → show 50 pairs of 101 → arrive at 5,050 → connect the method to the teacher and in-person academy → end card with website and enquiry CTA.

**Look:** Math Academy's paper, deep green, ink black and red palette; one restrained grid and crisp mathematical notation. No fake dashboard or generic feature cards.

**Sound:** intentionally silent in this environment; no source track or licensed music is available.

## 2. Alternative — The teacher's board

A real portrait against paper/ink fields introduces teacher Pranto Rai; a single real mathematics example is written and paired in sequence; return to the portrait and a contact end card. The structure feels more personal and teacher-led, with less abstract motion.

## 3. Alternative — One line, four discoveries

A single mathematical line travels through four simple geometric constructions to demonstrate “ছবি → যুক্তি → অনুশীলন”; the real teacher portrait and brand appear only after the visual proof. More experimental, but less immediately specific to SSC exam learners.

## Selection

Chosen: **An equation becomes an idea**. It opens on a familiar learner problem and proves a true result instead of listing features or making unsupported outcome claims.
