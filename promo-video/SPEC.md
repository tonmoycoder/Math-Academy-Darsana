# SPEC — 25-second promotional film

## One thing

A learner can understand a sum by seeing its structure: pair the first and last numbers, rather than adding every number one by one.

## Source-backed facts

- Homepage teaching idea: “গণিত মুখস্থ নয় — এটা যুক্তির ভাষা” and “ছবি → যুক্তি → অনুশীলন”.
- The academy offers offline mathematics batches in Darshana, Chuadanga for class 6–10/SSC; the active class, schedule, fee and seat availability must be confirmed with the academy.
- The site identifies Pranto Rai as teacher/founder. Portrait and lockup are supplied brand assets.
- Arithmetic proof: 1 + 2 + … + 100 = (1+100) + (2+99) + … = 50 × 101 = 5,050.

## Visual system

- Canvas: 1920×1080, 30 fps; keep key copy inside the 130 px horizontal / 140 px vertical inset, with all essential copy clear of the decorative border.
- Ground: warm paper `#FDFBF7`; primary ink `#111111`; deep green `#1A4D3E`; red accent `#D42C24`.
- Typography: local Noto Sans Bengali WOFF2; large Bengali display copy, simple high-contrast supporting copy.
- The real numerical relationship is the story's travelling object. Draw only arithmetic/geometry; do not imitate the academy website or invent controls/results.
- Use supplied original teacher image and academy identity assets, not AI substitutions.
